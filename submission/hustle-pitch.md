# 20-second Hustle Pitch

**Spoken script (about 45 words):**

> “Your income can change every week. Rent does not. Boloka helps gig workers see a cash shortfall before it arrives. Log a payout, add the bill, and get a clear weekly set-aside suggestion with the reason behind it. No bank password needed. Boloka: save, before you have to.”

**Shot list:**

1. 0–4s: speaker to camera; “Your income can change every week. Rent does not.”
2. 4–12s: phone capture of adding a synthetic payout and recurring rent.
3. 12–17s: show the forecast, confidence and plain-language explanation.
4. 17–20s: return to speaker and show the Boloka name and tagline.

Record with the actual app only after the deployed demo has passed the runbook. Do not show real personal data or imply that the forecast guarantees an outcome.
