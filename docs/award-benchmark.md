# App of the Year benchmark

Reviewed 24 October 2026.

## What the official material says

- The [Financial Solution category](https://appoftheyear.co.za/app-categories/) is for locally developed apps focused on the financial sector.
- The current [hackathon programme](https://appoftheyear.co.za/live-stream/) explicitly calls for user value, focus, adoption, and a pitch that shows the user, problem, product, evidence, impact, and ask. It also calls out cloud readiness, demo permissions, privacy, cyber safety, accessibility, AI disclosure, and third-party data use.
- The [past winners page](https://appoftheyear.co.za/past-winners/) features established, clearly explained products such as Checkers Sixty60 and financial products including Easy Equities and StokFella. The site describes Sixty60's practical grocery delivery promise and StokFella's support for informed financial decisions in stokvels.

## How Boloka responds

1. **One focused user problem:** a gig worker may receive money irregularly while rent and other bills remain predictable.
2. **A visible product action:** record cash flow, see the expected gap, and get a weekly set-aside suggestion with a plain-language reason.
3. **Honest evidence:** demonstrate the complete flow with synthetic entries, label all sample data, and do not present forecast quality or user outcomes as proven.
4. **Trust and safety:** no bank credentials or account aggregation; explain the estimate and confidence; keep service credentials server-side; restrict rows to the authenticated user.
5. **Adoption before feature volume:** test the core weekly habit with gig workers and community partners before adding more modules.

## Evidence still needed

- At least five observed user walkthroughs with permission and notes.
- A pilot with consent, a defined baseline, and a short follow-up measuring whether users record transactions and set money aside.
- Forecast calibration on a larger, consented dataset before making accuracy claims.
- A deployed, permission-checked demo and a backup screen recording.

These are recommendations based on the published material, not claims that the judges use a specific unpublished scoring rubric.
